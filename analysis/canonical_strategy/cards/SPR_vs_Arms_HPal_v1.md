# Shadow Priest / Subtlety Rogue vs Arms Warrior / Holy Paladin

Evidence: 80 games · 66W/14L/0 unknown result · medium confidence · provisional · v1

**Start:** Arms Warrior; recorded in 69/80 games. This is the labeled opening target, not necessarily the first sustained coordinated damage.  
**Objective:** Develop Warrior pressure and a usable conversion.  
**Win condition:** A vulnerable enemy can be pressured through limited partner support.  
**Create it:** Pressure Warrior; use the response to choose the next target.  
**Convert:** Control Warrior and commit Paladin, or finish a low Warrior.  
**Alternative:** After bubble: return Warrior or renew Paladin after immunity ends.  
**Failure mode:** A bubble-stopped low is never recreated before a friendly dies.  
**Reset:** Stabilise and renew; recheck current cooldowns. Both trinkets are not mandatory.
