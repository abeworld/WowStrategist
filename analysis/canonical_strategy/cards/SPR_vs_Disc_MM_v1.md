# Shadow Priest / Subtlety Rogue vs Discipline Priest / Marksmanship Hunter

Evidence: 13 games · 11W/2L/0 unknown result · low confidence · provisional · v1

**Start:** Marksmanship Hunter; recorded in 9/13 games. This is the labeled opening target, not necessarily the first sustained coordinated damage.  
**Objective:** Develop Hunter health pressure through absorbs and healing.  
**Win condition:** Renewed damage persists through healer disruption or a late interrupt.  
**Create it:** Usually start Hunter; some records start pet or Priest.  
**Convert:** Usually finish Hunter; nine of eleven wins identify that kill.  
**Alternative:** Absorbed/healed Hunter pressure can lead to a Priest switch.  
**Failure mode:** Control ends before real damage starts while friendly Rogue is already low.  
**Reset:** Recover and renew; Vanish alone did not guarantee survival.
