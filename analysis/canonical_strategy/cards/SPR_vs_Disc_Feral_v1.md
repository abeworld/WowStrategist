# Shadow Priest / Subtlety Rogue vs Discipline Priest / Feral Druid

Evidence: 31 games · 29W/2L/0 unknown result · medium confidence · provisional · v1

**Start:** Feral Druid; recorded in 25/31 games. This is the labeled opening target, not necessarily the first sustained coordinated damage.  
**Objective:** Develop Feral pressure with Priest disruption.  
**Win condition:** Feral stays low, or Feral control opens a Priest damage window.  
**Create it:** Pressure Feral; renew short healer disruption around health loss.  
**Convert:** Usually finish Feral through partial heals.  
**Alternative:** Healed Feral pressure can lead to a controlled-Feral Priest swap.  
**Failure mode:** Heals/Cyclone break damage before the team survives another attempt.  
**Reset:** Renew Feral pressure or the supported healer switch.

Priest/Rogue responsibilities: see the strategy object; unknown unless explicitly supported.
